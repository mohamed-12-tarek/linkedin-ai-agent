Introduction

OPT AI Agent ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use OPT AI Agent and connect your LinkedIn account.

Information We Collect

When you authorize OPT AI Agent through LinkedIn OAuth, we only access the permissions that you explicitly grant.

Depending on the permissions you approve, we may access information necessary to:

  • Authenticate your LinkedIn account.
  • Publish AI-generated content on your behalf.
  • Display your LinkedIn account connection status.

We do not collect any information beyond what is required to provide these services.

How We Use Your Information

Information obtained through LinkedIn APIs is used solely to:

  • Authenticate users.
  • Publish approved AI-assisted content.
  • Maintain your LinkedIn connection.
  • Improve the reliability and security of our services.

Data Security

Sensitive credentials, OAuth tokens, and related authentication data are encrypted and stored securely using industry-standard encryption practices.

We implement appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Third-Party Services

OPT AI Agent integrates with LinkedIn solely through LinkedIn's official APIs. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Your Rights

You may revoke OPT AI Agent's access to your LinkedIn account at any time through your LinkedIn account settings.

Once access is revoked, the application will no longer be able to publish content or access your LinkedIn account.
