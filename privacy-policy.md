# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 2026

## Introduction

OPT AI Agent ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use OPT AI Agent and connect your LinkedIn account.

## Information We Collect

When you authorize OPT AI Agent through LinkedIn OAuth, we only access the permissions that you explicitly grant.

Depending on the permissions you approve, we may access information necessary to:

- Authenticate your LinkedIn account
- Publish AI-generated content on your behalf
- Display your LinkedIn account connection status

We do **not** collect any information beyond what is required for these purposes.

## How We Use Your Information

Information obtained through LinkedIn APIs is used solely to:

- Authenticate users
- Publish approved content
- Display account connection status

Your information is never used for advertising, profiling, or sold to third parties.

## Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties.

Your information is only processed as required to provide the functionality of OPT AI Agent.

## Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information.

Sensitive credentials, OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, and other confidential data are encrypted and stored securely.

## User Control

You may revoke OPT AI Agent's access to your LinkedIn account at any time through your LinkedIn account settings.

Revoking access immediately prevents future interactions between OPT AI Agent and your LinkedIn account.

## Third-Party Services

OPT AI Agent may interact with third-party services, including but not limited to:

- LinkedIn

- AI model providers (such as Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, or OpenAI)

Each service operates under its own privacy policy and terms of service.
